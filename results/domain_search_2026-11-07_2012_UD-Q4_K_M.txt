# Speed of light

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

...

The speed of light in vacuum, commonly denoted c, is a universal physical constant exactly equal to 299,792,458 metres per second (approximately 300,000 kilometres per second). It is exact because, by international agreement, a metre is defined as the length of the path travelled by light in vacuum during a time interval of 1/299792458 of a second.
