### dict_memory
Persistent key-value store for this session.
Arguments:
- **operation**: `add` | `update` | `retrieve` | `retrieve_all` | `remove` | `remove_all`
- **dict**: object. Required for `add` and `update`.
- **key_list**: array of strings. Required for `retrieve` and `remove`.

Outputs:
- add: `Successfully added items of [<keys>] to memory.`
- update: `Successfully updated items of [<keys>] in memory.`
- retrieve: one line per key, `<key>: <value>`. Missing keys use `<key>: Key not found`. Return the exact value stored earlier.
- remove: `Successfully removed items of [<keys>] from memory.`
- remove_all: `Successfully cleared all memory.`

`web_search` and `web_extractor` keep the argument and output formats defined above.
